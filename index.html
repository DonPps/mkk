FAQ

Questions we are asked most.

Is MKK the provider of these programs?

No. MKK is the exclusive authorized agent of Respect Services in Morocco. All programs, applications, and service agreements are provided directly by Respect Services.

In which languages can I be advised?

Arabic and French, face to face, in Morocco.

Can MKK guarantee an outcome?

No. MKK does not issue citizenship or residency, does not approve applications, and does not guarantee outcomes. Decisions rest with the authorities of each program.

What does the first consultation cost?

The first consultation is free.
